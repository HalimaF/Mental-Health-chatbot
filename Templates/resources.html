If you're supporting someone else

Ask directly and calmly whether they are thinking about suicide — research is clear that asking does not plant the idea, and it often brings relief. Listen without rushing to fix it. Help them make contact with a professional rather than making the call for them where you can. Remove access to means if it is safe to do so. And take care of yourself too: carrying this alone is heavy.

Helpline numbers change. If one does not connect, please try another or call emergency services directly.
