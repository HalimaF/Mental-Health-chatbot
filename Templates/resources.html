If you're worried about someone else

  1. Ask directly. "Are you thinking about suicide?" Research is clear that asking does not plant the idea — it usually brings relief.
  2. Listen without fixing. You don't need the right answer. Staying calm and not flinching is the help.
  3. Help them make contact. Sit with them while they call, rather than calling on their behalf.
  4. Reduce access to means if it's safe for you to do so.
  5. Look after yourself too. Carrying someone else's crisis is heavy, and you're allowed to need support for it.
Helpline numbers change over time. If one doesn't connect, please try another or call emergency services directly.
